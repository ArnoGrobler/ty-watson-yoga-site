On Being · Essay Autumn: To Cohere in Mysterious Unity Parker J. Palmer
A question for the week Deaccessioning is how a museum, library or archive formally and permanently lets an object go from its collection. The reasons are usually simple. It no longer fits the mission. It is damaged beyond repair. It duplicates something already held. It turns out to be false. Or it was never really theirs to keep. What might each of us deaccession during this season to move into winter a little lighter?
